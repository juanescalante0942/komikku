## Design Specificity Verdict

Komikku has an authored reading-first editorial identity on Home, Details, and Reader, but major routes still fall back to interchangeable dark-card UI. The automated detector found no mechanical design violations in `src/app`; the source review nevertheless verified shared interaction and consistency gaps. Browser inspection was unavailable, so no live overlays or touch evidence was collected.

## What's Working

- Home copy, restrained red accent, and carousel establish a manga-specific editorial voice.
- Details has a clear content hierarchy from identity through chapter selection.
- Reader continuity is strong: persisted progress, continue-reading history, chapter navigation, and end-of-chapter actions.

## Priority Issues

### P1: Modal-like search and filter overlays are inaccessible

`Header.tsx:246-351` and `Library.tsx:393-426` lack dialog semantics, Escape close, focus trap, and focus restoration. Keyboard and assistive-tech users can lose their place or tab behind the overlay. Use an accessible dialog implementation and preserve the opener focus.

### P1: Reader and global mobile navigation compete for the same bottom edge

The global mobile navbar is fixed at `z-50` in `globals.css:111-130`, while reader controls occupy the same bottom location at `z-40` in `manga/[id]/[chapter]/page.tsx`. Let the reader own that safe area or suppress global nav on reader routes.

### P1: MangAI is orphaned from product navigation

Primary navigation exposes Home, Library, and Favorites, while MangAI is only reachable by direct route. Either make it a first-class discovery path or integrate recommendations into Library/Search.

### P2: Request failures impersonate empty states

`Library.tsx:136-142` and `Search.tsx:139-144` clear data on failure and render no-results states. Preserve the query/filter state, name the failure, and offer Retry.

### P2: System consistency and mobile touch targets are incomplete

Search, Author, and MangAI use legacy zinc/card treatments outside the newer token language. Several controls are under the 44px touch floor, including carousel arrows, header search, Library view/close controls, Favorites controls, and chapter pagination. Migrate route styling and normalize target sizing.
